# Diceri: the Google Meet alternative where you talk and listen in your own language

Everyone in the meeting hears your voice in their language.

Diceri is a video-call app with live voice translation. You speak your language; the other
person hears you, a moment later, in theirs, and you hear them in yours. No subtitles to read
and no interpreter to book.

- **Your own room.** Claim an @ and your room lives at `diceri.com/@you`. Guests join from
  the link without an account; you decide whether they walk in or ask first.
- **Spoken, not subtitled.** Each listener picks the language they want to hear; every phrase
  is transcribed, translated and spoken, with live captions alongside.
- **Free to start.** 60 call minutes a month, renewed on the 1st.
- **Open source.** AGPL-3.0, at <https://github.com/julianosig/diceri.com>.

Open your room at <https://diceri.com/>. More for agents: <https://diceri.com/llms.txt>.
